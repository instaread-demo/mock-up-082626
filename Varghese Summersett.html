Varghese Summersett Named to Inc. 5000 List for the Fourth Time

Varghese Summersett has once again been recognized as one of the fastest-growing private companies in America, earning a place on the prestigious 2026 Inc. 5000 list. This marks the fourth time the Texas law firm has received the national honor, underscoring its sustained growth, innovation, and commitment to serving clients across the state.

In this year’s rankings, released on August 11, Varghese Summersett placed No. 1,336 nationally, with a 263% three-year revenue growth. The firm also ranked No. 149 in Texas; No. 55 in the Dallas-Fort Worth area; and No. 40 among law firms nationwide.

Published annually by Inc. magazine, the Inc. 5000 recognizes the nation’s fastest-growing privately held companies and celebrates organizations that have demonstrated exceptional growth, resilience, and entrepreneurial success.

2026 Inc. 5000 Rankings

“Earning a place on the Inc. 5000 for the fourth time reflects something we’re especially proud of — sustained growth built the right way,” said Benson Varghese, founder and managing partner of Varghese Summersett. “Growth is never the goal by itself. It’s the result of building a team of exceptional people, embracing innovation, and staying focused on serving our clients. Every year brings new challenges, and we’re proud to continue evolving while remaining true to the values that have guided us from the very beginning.”

Founded in 2014 as a criminal defense practice operating out of a one-room office, Varghese Summersett has since grown into one of Texas’ premier law firms, with thriving criminal defense, family law, and personal injury divisions serving clients throughout the state. The firm now has offices in Fort Worth, Dallas, Southlake, and Houston, along with a team of more than 70 attorneys and legal professionals.

In addition to leading Varghese Summersett, Varghese is the founder of Lawft, a legal technology platform designed to help modern law firms operate more efficiently. He is also the author of Tapped In: Lessons for Law Firm Growth, a guide for attorneys seeking to build, grow, and scale successful law practices.

This is the firm’s fourth appearance on the Inc. 5000, following previous recognitions in 2025, 2024, and 2018.

“Our continued growth reflects the value we provide to our clients and our commitment to raising the standard for what they should expect from their law firm,” said Anna Summersett, co-owner and named partner. “We are proud of what our team has accomplished, and we remain focused on delivering exceptional service and results for the people who trust us to help them through some of life’s most difficult moments.”

According to Inc., this year’s list highlights companies that have driven significant economic growth despite an ever-changing business landscape.

About Varghese Summersett

Varghese Summersett is a premier personal injury, criminal defense, and family law firm in Texas dedicated to helping people through life’s greatest challenges. Each division is full of experienced, award-winning attorneys committed to providing exceptional legal services. Varghese Summersett has been named a DFW Favorite, Best Law Firm, Readers’ Choice, Small Business of the Year, Best Company to Work For, and Best Place for Working Parents, among other accolades. The firm’s lawyers have also been recognized as Top Attorneys, Super Lawyers, Rising Stars, Best Lawyers in America, and Ones to Watch in America. Five attorneys are also board-certified, the highest designation an attorney can reach. The firm’s headquarters is in Fort Worth, but it also has locations in Dallas, Southlake, and Houston. Learn more at versustexas.com.

About the Inc. 5000

The Inc. 5000 ranks privately held U.S. companies based on percentage revenue growth over a three-year period. Companies must meet strict revenue and eligibility requirements and successfully complete Inc.’s editorial review process to be included on the annual list. Additional information about the methodology and this year’s honorees is available at www.inc.com/inc5000.
