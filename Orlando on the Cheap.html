Central Florida Fall Festival at Amber Brooke Farms, Eustis

POSTED BY
Elaine
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:09
Instaread

For the 6th year, Amber Brooke Farms in Eustis will hold the Central Florida Fall Festival to celebrate the autumn season.

The festival offers lots of family-friendly farm activities, including a pumpkin patch, a jumping pillow, a corn maze, a petting zoo, and more farm-themed activities. Enjoy the beautiful zinnia and sunflower fields, tractor rides, live music, food trucks, and photo ops. 

What to Know

Amber Brooke Farms
36111 N CR 44A Eustis, FL 32736

  • Dates: Monday, October 12 and Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays, September 19 to November 22, 2026
  • Price: Ages 2 and under are always FREE.
    • Value Admission: Online: $19.95 Gate: $22.95
      • Value Dates: Sept. 19, 20, 26, 27
        Oct. 2, 3, 4, 9, 16, 23, 30
        Nov. 7, 8, 14, 15, 21, 22
    • Peak Admission: Online: $21.95 Gate: $24.95
      • Peak Dates: Oct. 10, 11, 12, 17, 18, 24, 25, 31
        Nov. 1
