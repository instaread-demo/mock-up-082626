Man arrested in Kelowna charged with multiple intimate partner violence offences

Published 3:47 pm Tuesday, August 25, 2026

By Ty Lim

Man being arrested in front of Moxies at 1700-block of Cooper Road on Monday, Aug. 24. (Syrinna Van Hoorn/Facebook)

A man accused of committing violence offences against an intimate partner was brought into custody during what police are calling a “high risk” arrest at a local restaurant.

During the afternoon of Monday, Aug. 24, Trevor Smithen, 42, was arrested in front of Moxies’ Kelowna restaurant at the 1700-block of Cooper Road. The arrest was conducted by members of the Kelowna RCMP along with the Southeast District Emergency Response Team (ERT) – who where there “out of an abundance of caution” ensuring public safety.

“The coordinated police response resulted in a high-risk arrest being carried out safely,” said Cpl. Steven Lang, Kelowna RCMP Media Relations Officer.

The next day, on Aug. 25, Smithen appeared in the BC Provincial Court in Kelowna, where the following charges were approved against him: forcible confinement; assault; assault with a weapon; violence used, threatened, or attempted against an intimate partner; uttering threats to cause death or bodily harm, and breach of a release order.

According to the courts, the offences Smithen is accused of occurred on Aug. 18.

Lang said Smithen remains in custody pending his next court appearance, which is scheduled for Aug. 26.
